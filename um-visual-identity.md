# Maastricht University Visual Identity Guidelines

> Last updated: August 2026  
> Source: UM Brandbook / Communicatiegids / House Style Office

---

## 1. Logo

### Primary Logo
The UM logo features **two vertically aligned, slightly offset blue triangles** enclosing the letters 'U' and 'M'. This iconic design was unveiled in **January 1999** and will be retained in the current visual identity refresh.

### Logo Variations
- **Standard**: Blue triangles on white/light backgrounds
- **Reversed**: White triangles on dark backgrounds
- **UM-MUMC+ Collaboration**: Logo order depends on dominant party in the collaboration

### Language Variants
The wordmark beside the triangles is language-specific and must follow the language of
the surrounding interface:

| Language | Wordmark | Asset |
|---|---|---|
| English | Maastricht University | `assets/um-logo-en-blue.svg` |
| Dutch | Universiteit Maastricht | `assets/um-logo-nl-blue.svg` |

### Logo Usage Rules
- Never distort, rotate, or modify the logo proportions
- Maintain clear space around the logo (minimum = height of the "U")
- Never place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast

removed from the tool in favour of a flat soft-blue canvas.

## 3. Typography

### Primary Font: **GT Standard**
GT Standard is the brand font of the 2025 refresh, used for both headlines and body
text. It replaces **Fort**, which the pre-refresh identity specified.

GT Standard is licensed. Serving the webfont is covered on UM-owned domains
(this tool ships on `tools.maastrichtuniversity.nl`); it must not be used on
third-party platforms without checking the licence first.

### Optical Sizes
GT Standard ships in three optical sizes — **S**, **M**, **L** — which track the size
text is *rendered at*, not whether it is a heading. Pick by rendered size:

| Optical size | Use for |
|---|---|
| **L** | Display type, roughly 22px and above |
| **M** | Mid-range text |
| **S** | Body copy, labels, and UI text below 22px |

### Available Weights
The supplied webfont package contains **Regular (400)** and **Bold (700)** only —
no Light, Medium, SemiBold, or ExtraBold. Design to those two weights; anything else
renders as a browser-synthesised face.

### Fallback Font: **Aptos**
Where GT Standard is unavailable — Microsoft Office applications in particular — use
**Aptos** as the system font.

**Why Aptos:**
- High legibility in text-heavy documents
- Clean, contemporary appearance
- Slight warmth in design
- Native to Windows/Microsoft 365

On the web, the fallback stack is `system-ui, sans-serif`.

### Typography Usage Hierarchy
1. **Headlines**: GT Standard L Bold (or Aptos Bold)
2. **Subheadings**: GT Standard L Bold
3. **Body Text**: GT Standard S Regular (or Aptos Regular)
4. **Captions/Footnotes**: GT Standard S Regular at smaller sizes

### Font Sizing Guidelines
- **H1 Headlines**: 32-48pt
- **H2 Subheadings**: 24-28pt
- **Body Text**: 11-14pt
- **Captions**: 9-10pt

## 4. Design Principles

### Border Radius
- **Cards/UI elements**: `32px` border-radius
- Buttons and containers follow this rounded corner aesthetic

### Spacing & Layout
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Use the stone shapes subtly as background texture
- Keep content aligned to a clear grid system

### Photography Style
- Authentic, candid imagery preferred
- Show real students, staff, and campus life
- Avoid generic stock photography
- Natural lighting over studio setups

## 5. Transparency & Disclosure

### When to Disclose AI Involvement
| Content Type | Disclosure Required? | Placement |
|--------------|----------------------|-----------|
| Press Release (AI-drafted) | Yes | Footer note |
| Social Media Post (AI-drafted) | Optional | Alt text or comments |
| Translation (AI-assisted) | No | Internal log only |
| Image (AI-generated) | Yes | Image caption |
| Crisis Communication | N/A | No AI used |
| Executive Statement | N/A | No AI used |

### Standard Disclosure Language
- "Drafted with AI assistance, reviewed and approved by [Name]"
- "AI-generated image"
- "Translation: AI-assisted with human review"
